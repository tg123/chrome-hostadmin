Syntax rules

Mapping

[#]<IP> <HOSTNAME> [<HOSTNAME>...] [# COMMENT]

A leading # disables the mapping. A comment containing the word hide keeps the line out of the popup. 

127.0.0.1 localhost # hide

Groups

#==== Project 1
#127.0.0.1  localhost1
127.0.0.1   localhost2
#====

Clicking a group in the popup enables or disables all of its mappings at once. Add # hide after the group name to hide the whole group.

Bulk hide

#hide_all_of_below
…everything below is ignored by the popup…
